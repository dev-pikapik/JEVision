The first release of JEVision Remote.

• Pair the iPhone with JEVision on your Mac by scanning a QR code and confirming with Face ID.
• Browse projects and tests, start a test or one row of its data, and follow the run block by block.
• Edit block settings, add or delete blocks, and rename tests right from the iPhone.
• Read reports with a short reason for each failure, and look through the run history.
• See test cards, sticky notes and runs that are waiting for the screen.
• The Mac decides whether each iPhone may view, run or edit tests.
• The link to the Mac is end-to-end encrypted, and the iPhone signs in with its own hardware-protected key.
• No Mac at hand? Look around the built-in example.
• Use the app in English or Russian, and at the largest text sizes.
