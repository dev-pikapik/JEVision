JEVision Remote is the iPhone remote for JEVision, a free, open-source Mac app that tests the user interface of Mac apps. The tests run on the Mac; the iPhone browses them, starts runs, follows them block by block and shows the reports.

HOW TO REVIEW THE APP WITHOUT A MAC
1. Open the app and tap "See an Example" at the bottom of the first screen. No Face ID, permission or network is needed for the example.
2. "Example Mac" appears in the list of Macs. Tap it, then the project "Acme Mail".
3. Tap "Greet every person": the test is drawn as a flowchart, with its card and sticky notes (tap a note to read it whole). Tap "Run on Example Mac" and follow the run block by block; it passes in about 10 seconds. "Stop" stops a run.
4. Go back, open the folder "login", then "Sign in with valid credentials", and run it. It fails at its last check; "Show Report" says why, and tapping the failed step shows the screen the Mac saw and the picture it expected.
5. "Runs" (top right of the project) lists the run history. The gear on the Macs list opens Settings: the app lock and the About links.
6. "Close Example" at the top of the Macs list returns to the first screen. Nothing from the example is saved.

WHAT A REAL MAC NEEDS
To use the app for real you need a Mac with JEVision 1.0 or later, which needs macOS 26 or later. JEVision for Mac is free, open source, and distributed through GitHub Releases, not the Mac App Store: https://github.com/dev-pikapik/JEVision/releases
On the Mac, JEVision Settings → iPhone & Remote shows a pairing code. The iPhone scans it and the owner confirms with Face ID or the passcode.

PERMISSIONS AND WHY
- Camera: only to scan the pairing code shown on the Mac. The picture is read on the iPhone and is neither saved nor sent.
- Local Network: to reach the paired Mac directly on the same Wi-Fi. When that is not possible, the end-to-end encrypted connection goes through a relay server that can't read it.
- Face ID: to lock the app and to confirm pairing. The key that proves the iPhone to the Mac lives in the Secure Enclave and works only after Face ID or the passcode.
Each permission is asked for only when it is first needed: the camera when you tap "Scan Pairing Code", Face ID when you pair or unlock, the local network when the app first connects to a Mac.

ACCOUNTS
There is no account and no sign-in, so there is no demo account. The app has no in-app purchases, ads, tracking or analytics, and nothing in it talks to a server of ours.
